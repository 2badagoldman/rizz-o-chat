# Complete Instagram-style Stories rail

## What will change
- Turn Stories into a full-width, edge-to-edge horizontal strip on Home and Discover.
- Keep many creator profiles in one uninterrupted row with smooth touch dragging, mouse-wheel scrolling, and scroll snapping.
- Add clear previous/next controls on larger screens without covering story photos.
- Preserve “Your story,” unseen/seen rings, creator names, story viewing, posting, and replies.
- Expand demo story coverage so the rail does not stop after only a few creators.

## Validation
- Check the rail on mobile and desktop.
- Confirm the final story is reachable, each profile opens its own stories, and no content is clipped or overlapping.
